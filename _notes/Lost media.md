As someone who researches film, it also would serve me well to know how to find media which has not been published on popular channels. Also, as someone specifically researching film materiality, I want to track the remaining traces of nitrate film published online. Nitrate film is highly flammable and due to this condition, it has a propensity to combust. Thus, roughly 75% of American silent films have been lost forever, [according to the American Film Institute](https://www.afi.com/news/afi-catalog-spotlight-the-recent-discovery-of-a-lost-silent-film/).

Film decays and becomes brittle. The emulsion bubbles until the image appears as if it has [rotten](https://www.filmcare.org/vd_binder.php). This is accepted and accredited to the fragility of physical objects. However, I believe we have to be prepared for the [inevitable decay of digital media](https://www.getty.edu/news/preventing-digital-decay/).
